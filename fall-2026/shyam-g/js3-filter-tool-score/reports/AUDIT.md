## What the random-reject sample can and cannot show

- 0 of 27 randomly drawn rejects were wrong by my hand verdict (unsure excluded).
- 95% exact (Clopper-Pearson) interval for the false-reject share **among the 624 rejects of this one corpus**: 0.0% to 12.8%.
- In postings: roughly 0 to 80 of the 624 rejects could be relevant. This is a sample estimate, not a measured rate.
- It does **not** describe other boards, other dates, or another persona. It is also dominated by easy location rejects (New York, London, D.C.), so it says little about the hard rejects. That's why the non-location stratum and the close calls are judged separately and are not pooled into the random-sample figure.
